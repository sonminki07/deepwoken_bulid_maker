# 📚 Deepwoken Wiki: Enchantments (인챈트 (전체))
> **총 항목 수**: 55개 | **출처**: https://deepwoken.co/wiki/enchantment

---

### Astral
- **설명**: Grants +20% weapon damage in PvE. Call down stars alongside your critical attacks, and afer parrying thrice.

### Blazing
- **설명**: Once enough stacks have been applied, for 5 seconds your attacks will ignite your foes.

### Bluster
- **설명**: After landing a few stacks of Bluster, start a countdown to an explosion that scales off of your M1 damage. Land attacks during this countdown to make it explode faster. This explosion scales with your weapon investment.

### Chilling
- **설명**: Chill your foes. Applies Bottom Freeze once enough stacks have been applied.

### Deferred
- **설명**: Weapon damage is increased by 7%, but becomes damage over time, lasting for 2.5 seconds.

### Detonation
- **설명**: Once enough stacks have been applied, an explosion is detonated.

### Elastic
- **설명**: Opponents are pulled towards you on hit.

### Erebus
- **설명**: When landing a critical, make your opponent forget how to use a mantra for a minute.

### Gluttony
- **설명**: Steals Water and Stomach from those you hit.

### Grim
- **설명**: Once enough stacks have been applied, Grim is applied for 6 seconds, increasing all incoming damage by 25% and all incoming posture damage by 50%.

### Harrowing
- **설명**: Obscures your opponent's health, posture, and armor bars. Also causes PvE to get Confusion on successful hits.

### Heroism
- **설명**: When above 75% HP, gain 4% damage for every 5% HP above 75%. When below 25% HP, gain 4% damage for every 5% HP below 25% HP. You can reflect negative status effects with light attacks or criticals to your opponent.

### Kyrsan Chill
- **설명**: On critical hits, apply a slow that increases with each crit. At 5 stacks fully encase your opponent in ice.

### Metal
- **설명**: Steal Armor durability on hit.

### Nemesis
- **설명**: Your critical attack is replaced with Nemesis. Basic attacks now mark your enemies, allowing you to critical attack to teleport to them.

### Nightbringer
- **설명**: 설명 없음

### Obfuscation
- **설명**: You gain some chip damage on m1 and crit.

### Parasol's Blight
- **설명**: Hitting a player infects their body with Parasol's Blight. If a player who is infected is killed, their corpse is turned into one of the Parasol's Minions. This also has a chance of proccing on PvE enemies you kill.

### Providence Thorns
- **설명**: Parrying attacks damages your foes.

### Sear
- **설명**: Weapon gains 5% PEN. Applies Cauterize on hit for 15s, as well as draining their Water. Cauterized foes take more PEN the more stacks are applied.

### Solar
- **설명**: Applies a Solar Mark on critical attack or flourish. Solar Marks last 8s and reduce Tempo gain and Ether regeneration. Your Vents drain the Tempo of those you've marked.

### Stone
- **설명**: Do extra true damage whenever you guardbreak an opponent with a light attack or critical.

### Storm
- **설명**: Shock your foes on hit every 5s. Rain will upgrade this shock into a lightning bolt called down from above.

### Stormbreaker
- **설명**: Your critical attack is replaced with Stormbreaker Hurl, alloying you to launch your weapon. Shock your foes on hit every 5s. Occasionally, a lightning bolt may be called down from above.

### Suffocated
- **설명**: Landing a critical, uppercut, flourish, or offhand attack procs suffocation.

### Tears of the Edenkite
- **설명**: On critical attacks and flourishes, produce homing bubble projectiles. 20s CD.

### Umbral Knight
- **설명**: On hit, the Umbral Knight will strike your foes for 20% of your weapon's damage after a 0.25s delay. 5s CD.

### Vampirism
- **설명**: Your weapon attacks drain health from your foes and replenish your blood.

### Windswept
- **설명**: After landing enough stacks, proc winded on your opponent. The duration scales with your weapon investment.

### Wild
- **설명**: On hit, deal 85% of your weapon's damage in an AoE. This AoE cannot hit allies or the enemy originally hit. Reduces your damage against allies by 50%.

### Curse of the Authoritative
- **설명**: On critical, uppercut, or flourish, apply Authoritative on your opponent making them take 50% more posture damage. When you get guardbroken, take 50% more damage.

### Curse of the Bloodthirsty
- **설명**: Deal +10% weapon damage and drain the blood of your foes, but suffer blood loss if your weapon attacks miss.

### Curse of Captain Greene
- **설명**: Known throughout the Summer Company, many heard of the famous treasurer Captrain Greene. You inherit his strange ability of gaining damage the more rich you are. However since you aren't Captain Greene himself, you lose notes whenever you get hit. A strange curse indeed...

### Curse of the No Life King
- **설명**: Grants passive regen and damages the sanity of your foes, but succumb to insanity much faster, lose twice as much blood, and never be more than a step away from death.

### Curse of Repulsion
- **설명**: When you block attacks, you knock your attacker backwards. You receive +30% chip damage.

### Curse of the Unbidden
- **설명**: Your critical attack is replaced with Unbidden Counter, restoring posture and stunning your attacker. Your attacks drain Ether on hit.

### Curse of Void
- **설명**: Every enemy you slay while equipping this weapon will always be Drowned. Enemies hit will also experience reduced healing. It also applies 10 seconds of Ether Sunder to PvE enemies.

### Curse of Ysley's Ember
- **설명**: Once enough stacks have been applied, the Fire of Life germinates. The explosion deals a random amount of damage and grants temporary health to the affected targets.

### Curse of Yun'Shul
- **설명**: Has a chance to inflict a curse that deals percentage damage, but who it falls upon is often down to luck.

### JOE's
- **설명**: It stands for Judicator's Original Enchantment. An enchant that was supposed to be only for the strongest of monster slayers. No one knows what happened to the original Judicator who made this enchant.

### Life Weave
- **설명**: Unknown.

### Tainted Sorrow
- **설명**: Unknown.

### Unstable
- **설명**: Unknown.

### Adhesive
- **설명**: Pulls your attacker towards you.

### Allure
- **설명**: Incoming hits may Charm your attacker for 10s.

### Bounce
- **설명**: Incoming hits may bounce your attacker away.

### Displacement
- **설명**: Incoming hits may proc Displacement, granting a speed boost and clearing all stuns present.

### Drowned
- **설명**: Bolster the stats of your equipment, adding an additional +10 Health, +10 Ether, +5% Damage vs. Monsters, and +5% Monster Armor.

### Entanglement
- **설명**: Incoming hits may proc Entangled Defence, reducing your speed in exchange for 10% damage reduction.

### Ferocity
- **설명**: Incoming hits may proc a speed boost and deal reduced damage. Both the speed boost and damage reduction scale with your current Tempo.

### Multiplicity
- **설명**: Incoming hits may grant you a random boon of Recovery, Healing, or Agility. 5s CD.

### Rain
- **설명**: Incoming hits may summon rain clouds above you.

### Stench
- **설명**: Incoming hits may produce a Stench Cloud, gradually draining 3% of their health if not blocked.

### Viscosity
- **설명**: Incoming damage is converted into damage over time applied for 2.5s.

### Ether Surge
- **설명**: Landing enough light attacks gives you infinite ether for some time.
