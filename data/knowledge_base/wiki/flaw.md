# 📚 Deepwoken Wiki: Flaws (결함/디버프 (전체))

---

### None
- **설명**: 설명 없음

### Blind
- **설명**: You cannot see.

### Deficient
- **설명**: You regenerate Ether more slowly.

### Fugitive
- **설명**: Your past isn't so spotless. All Factions hate you.

### Glutton
- **설명**: Your hunger and thirst is depleted faster.

### Haemophilia
- **설명**: You bleed more.

### Manic
- **설명**: All insanity gained from attacks is heavily amplified.

### Obvious
- **설명**: Enemies detect you more easily.

### Simple
- **설명**: You learn things at a slower rate than others.

### Squeamish
- **설명**: Having to physically take the life of another makes you lose stomach.

### Vegetarian
- **설명**: Eating the flesh of animals has a chance to make you vomit.
