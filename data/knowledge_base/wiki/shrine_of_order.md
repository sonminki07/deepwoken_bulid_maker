# 🏛️ Deepwoken Official Wiki: Shrine of Order (질서의 성소)

> **Source**: [Deepwoken Fandom Wiki - Deep Shrines/Shrine of Order](https://deepwoken.fandom.com/wiki/Deep_Shrines/Shrine_of_Order)
> **Cost**: 10 Knowledge | **Limit**: Once per character | **Requirements**: Power 8+, No Oath (Oathless allowed)

---

## 📍 Locations
1. **Songseeker Mines**: Deep through the mudskipper cavern passage.
2. **Temple of Hearts (Depths)**: First massive shrine on the left path to Shrine of Yun'Shul.

---

## ⚙️ Core Rules & Mechanics
1. **Stat Averaging**: Takes points from invested attributes above average and redistributes them into lower invested attributes.
2. **The -25 Point Loss Cap**: Core attributes and weapon stats **can never lose more than 25 points** from Shrine of Order. Attunement stats have no cap and can be reduced to 0.
3. **Racial Base Protection**: Innate racial starting points (e.g., Canor Str 3, Cha 2) are never deducted and serve as the absolute bottom floor.
4. **Permanent Pre-Shrine Talents**: Talents unlocked before ordering (Reinforced Armor, Ghost, Showstopper, Collapsed Lung, Underdog) remain active forever even after stats drop.
5. **Free Points Return**: Indivisible points during averaging are safely returned as free investment points.

---

## 🎯 Meta Pre-Shrine Strategy
- **Pre-Shrine (Power 8~15)**: Rush Fortitude 90 (Reinforced Armor), Agility 40 (Ghost), Charisma 15 (Tough Love), Willpower 30 (Underdog).
- **Post-Shrine (Power 16~20)**: Order stats and pour all recovered and remaining points into Weapon 100 and Attunement 80 for the ultimate 330pt endgame build.
