- **설명**: Doing things alone yields greater experience.

- **설명**: You have a much higher max carry load and can carry much larger quantities of food.

- **설명**: You gain an extra Ace every other Whisper Roll.

- **설명**: You sweet talk your way out of trouble, gaining less bad reputation from committing crimes.

### Steadfast
- **설명**: Your armor retains more durability when attacked.

### Survivalist
- **설명**: You are resistant to environmental effects.
