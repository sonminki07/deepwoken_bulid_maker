### Sudden Ambush
- **설명**: The Doom of Caeranthil dives underwater, then giant bubbles emerge beneath the player for a few seconds. After which, the Doom of Caeranthil then dives up and consumes any player caught in its vicinity, instantly killing them. It will then land in the water nearby, also dealing damage, but not instantly killing them.

### Pressure Eruption
- **설명**: The Doom of Caeranthil dives underwater, then summons multiple geysers underneath players that fling them into the air, potentially knocking them from their boat and dealing significant damage.

### Tidal Roar
- **설명**: The Doom of Caeranthil charges up before releasing a powerful roar that deals damage and stuns players who get too close. In addition, it summons multiple tidal waves that will damage boats and carry away swept enemies.

### Whirlpool
- **설명**: The Doom of Caeranthil swims repetitively in a circle, creating a massive whirlpool that continuously deals massive damage and ragdolls enemies into the air.

### Luminant Beam
- **설명**: The Doom of Caeranthil charges a bright green attack for 15-20 seconds before firing a massive laser that obliterates everything in its path.

### Barrel Roll
- **설명**: The Doom of Caeranthil will spin horizontally, knocking back players who are using their weapons and dealing a low amount of damage. In addition, the Doom of Caeranthil will bite enemies near its head.

### Pierce
- **설명**: When close enough, the Interluminary Parasol starts rapidly stabbing random targets with its arms, sometimes grabbing a player and rapidly draining their blood.

### Spawn of the Abyss
- **설명**: The Interluminary Parasol throws a boulder that deals damage on impact, summoning a Depths mob — either an Alpha Megalodaunt, Broodlord, Thresher, or Nautilodaunt.

### Vehemens Ventus
- **설명**: Charges for ~10 seconds then launches a large purple gale orb that deals massive posture and Wind damage on impact; avoid by air-dash, Ghost/Dark Rift, floor drop, waypoint, or portals.
