## 📝 빌드 개요 및 총평
A high-sustain, high-damage vampire-themed build utilizing the Starkindred Oath wings and Bloodrend attunement. It relies on heavy weapon criticals, bleed effects, and life drain while maintaining high mobility in PvP combat.

### ⚖️ 장점 및 단점
**장점**:
- ✅ High health regeneration and lifesteal via Bloodrend mantras
- ✅ Excellent mobility and air control using Starkindred wings
- ✅ Strong posture damage and heavy weapon burst potential
- ✅ Optimized stat distribution using Shrine of Order
**단점**:
- ⚠️ Requires precise execution of Shrine of Order stat allocation
- ⚠️ Reliant on maintaining tempo and landing Bloodrend skills
- ⚠️ Susceptible to heavy parry punish if attacks are mis-timed
