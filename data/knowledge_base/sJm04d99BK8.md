## 📝 빌드 개요 및 총평
A high-damage PvP build created around the Palace Tachi medium weapon. By combining high Charisma, Intelligence penetration talents, and the Contractor oath, the weapon's guardbreak criticals become extremely abusive and allow execute setups.

### ⚖️ 장점 및 단점
**장점**:
- ✅ Extremely high penetration stacking via Charisma and Intelligence talents
- ✅ Guaranteed guardbreak criticals synergy with Cornered Fool talent
- ✅ Contractor critical execution capability
- ✅ Large ether pool allowing massive mantra modifications
**단점**:
- ⚠️ Low base weapon swing speed
- ⚠️ Low Agility investment
