## 📝 빌드 개요 및 총평
Extremely tanky and high DPS PvE build featuring massive M1 scaling and posture break. Excellent mobility and utility for clearing Layer 2 and farming major bosses.

### ⚖️ 장점 및 단점
**장점**:
- ✅ Massive M1 and critical damage scaling via stacked damage buffs
- ✅ High posture breaking ability with Ironsing mantras to bypass monster armor
- ✅ Exceptional utility and safety with Soulbreaker murmurs (Tacet, Rhythm, Ardour) and Spark Swap
**단점**:
- ⚠️ Requires Shrine of Order and multiple stat investments to assemble
- ⚠️ Heavily optimized for PvE encounters rather than PvP fights
